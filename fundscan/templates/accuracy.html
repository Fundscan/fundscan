How close is the headline number to what actually happens?

Every FundScan figure is a snapshot: the net APY a pair is showing right now. Funding rates move, so a 45% headline can average 12% over a week. Rather than ask you to trust that the numbers are honest, this page publishes the comparison directly — current headline net APY against the realized average over the window, for every pair with enough history to mean something. Sorted by the largest gap first, not the best cases.

What this measures: "Realized average" is the plain mean of net APY across every snapshot collected for that pair in the window (funding rates are re-fetched every 60 seconds). "Gap" is current headline minus that average — positive means the headline is currently running hot versus its own recent history, negative means it's currently running cold. This is a measure of headline volatility, not a promise about future returns: funding rates can move sharply and stay unfavorable for extended periods regardless of the recent average.
